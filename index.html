Rally Replay for iPhone

Support

Rally Replay finds every rally in your pickleball videos on-device, lets you jump point to point, watch in slow motion, and save clips or a dead-time-free highlight reel to Photos.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or footage the detector struggled with: earlvlee@gmail.com. Attaching a short clip of the problem section helps enormously.

Common questions

  • It missed a rally. Tap the Mark button at the serve and again when the ball is dead — that adds the rally and teaches the detector. In Settings → Calibrate, "Fit detector to my labels" retunes it to your court.
  • It found rallies from the court next to me. Same fix: mark a few real ones and run the fit. Busy rec centers are the hardest case.
  • Do I need an OpenAI key? No. Detection, playback, clips, and the highlight reel all work offline. The key only powers the optional Analyze and Game Report features, and you're billed by OpenAI directly.
  • Where do clips go? Your Photos library, via add-only access. The app can't read your library.
