Rally Replay for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026

Rally Replay does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers operated by the developer.

Your videos

Videos you open stay on your device. Rally detection, playback, marking, calibration, and clip export all run entirely on your iPhone. Clips are saved to your photo library using add-only access; the app cannot read or browse your library.

Optional AI features

If you choose to add your own OpenAI API key in Settings, tapping Analyze or running a Game Report sends still frames from the selected rally and shot-timing data directly from your device to OpenAI's API under your key, governed by OpenAI's privacy policy. Nothing is sent until you explicitly start an analysis, and the app shows an estimated cost before each one. Your key is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device and is never sent anywhere other than OpenAI.

Data retention

Analysis results are cached on your device so you aren't billed twice for the same rally. Deleting the app deletes them. The developer never receives them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
